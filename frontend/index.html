Essential Women's Safety Rights

Key legal protections and guidelines every woman should know.

1. Zero FIR Right

A woman can file an FIR at any police station, irrespective of where the incident occurred or jurisdictional limits. The police must record it and transfer it later.

2. Arrest at Sunset Protection

According to Section 46(4) of the CrPC, a woman cannot be arrested after sunset and before sunrise, except in extraordinary circumstances with prior written permission of a judicial magistrate.

3. Right to Confidentiality in Harassment Cases

Victims of sexual harassment or violence have the right to keep their identity anonymous. Statements must be recorded in privacy or in the presence of a female police officer.

4. Free Legal Aid & Medical Assistance

Under the Legal Services Authorities Act, female victims are entitled to free legal aid, counseling, and immediate emergency medical care at all government and private hospitals without waiting for police reports.

Quick Street Safety Guidelines

  • Keep keys ready: Hold your key between index and middle finger when walking through deserted corridors or parking lots.
  • Walk facing traffic: Keeps you visible to drivers and prevents a vehicle from sneaking up behind you.
  • Trust your instincts: If a route or person feels off, cross the street immediately or step into a lighted open shop.
  • Share live trip: Use the WhatsApp alert button on this app to send your live location coordinates to your guardian before taking a cab late at night.
